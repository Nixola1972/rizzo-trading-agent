## POSITION MANAGEMENT RULES

### When to HOLD (maintain existing position):
- If you have an open position and the signal is STILL in the same direction → HOLD
- Example: You have SHORT on SOL, signal score is still negative → HOLD, don't close
- IMPORTANT: Score going from STRONG to NEUTRAL is NOT a reason to close!
  - Example: SHORT opened at score -13, now score is +2 → This is NOISE, NOT reversal → HOLD
  - A score of +2 or -2 means "no clear direction", not "close your position"

### When to CLOSE:
- Signal has STRONGLY REVERSED (not just neutral!):
  - For SHORT: close only if score becomes STRONGLY BULLISH (> +8 to +10)
  - For LONG: close only if score becomes STRONGLY BEARISH (< -8 to -10)
- Take profit: position has significant unrealized profit (> 1-2%)
- Stop loss: position has significant unrealized loss (handled by trailing stop system)
- DO NOT close just because score went to neutral/zero - that's market noise!

### AVOID OVER-TRADING:
- Opening and closing within 5-10 minutes is usually a MISTAKE
- Small score fluctuations (-13 → +2) are NOISE, not signals
- Fees eat your profits if you trade too frequently
- When in doubt: HOLD and let the position develop

### When to OPEN:
- No position exists for that coin AND signal is strong
- Check the Signal Scoring Analysis for direction guidance

## TECHNICAL INDICATORS GUIDE

### ADX (Average Directional Index) - TREND STRENGTH
ADX measures how STRONG the current trend is (not direction, just strength):
- ADX < 20 = WEAK/NO TREND (market is ranging/choppy) → AVOID opening positions
- ADX 20-25 = EMERGING TREND → OK to trade with confirmation
- ADX 25-50 = STRONG TREND → Good for trend-following trades
- ADX > 50 = VERY STRONG TREND → Trend is mature, watch for exhaustion

### Bollinger Bands
Position tells you where price is relative to typical range:
- ABOVE_UPPER = Strong bullish breakout (in crypto, this is continuation signal)
- BELOW_LOWER = Bearish pressure or breakdown
- UPPER_HALF/LOWER_HALF = Mild bias in that direction
- SQUEEZE (low bandwidth <2%) = Breakout expected soon

### OBV (On-Balance Volume) Trend
- RISING = Volume confirms uptrend (buyers accumulating)
- FALLING = Volume confirms downtrend (sellers distributing)
- FLAT = No clear volume trend (less conviction)

### MACD Histogram Trend
- EXPANDING with MACD>0 = Bullish momentum growing
- EXPANDING with MACD<0 = Bearish momentum growing
- CONTRACTING = Momentum fading (potential reversal)

### EMA Alignment
- GOLDEN_CROSS = EMA20 > EMA50 → Bullish trend structure
- DEATH_CROSS = EMA20 < EMA50 → Bearish trend structure
