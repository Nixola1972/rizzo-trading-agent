## MULTIPLE POSITIONS ALLOWED
You can have UP TO 3 POSITIONS open simultaneously (one per coin: BTC, ETH, SOL).
Each decision should focus on ONE coin at a time, but consider your overall portfolio exposure.

## OPERATIONS
- operation must be "open", "close", or "hold"
- direction must be "long" or "short"

### For "open": Open a new position
  - symbol: which coin to trade (BTC, ETH, or SOL)
  - direction: "long" or "short"
  - target_portion_of_balance: % of available cash to use (0.0-1.0), recommended 0.2-0.4 per position
  - leverage: leverage multiplier (1-10, higher = more risk/reward)

### For "close": Close an existing position
  - symbol: which coin position to close
  - direction: must match the position side you want to close ("long" or "short")
  - target_portion_of_balance can be omitted (closes entire position)

### For "hold": No action taken
  - Use when current positions should be maintained
  - direction can be omitted

## POSITION MANAGEMENT RULES

### When to HOLD (maintain existing position):
- If you have an open position and the signal is STILL in the same direction → HOLD
- Example: You have SHORT on SOL, signal score is still negative → HOLD, don't close
- Only close if: signal REVERSES or becomes clearly NEUTRAL

### When to CLOSE:
- Signal has REVERSED (was bearish, now bullish or vice versa)
- Signal has become NEUTRAL/WEAK (score near zero)
- Take profit: position has significant unrealized profit
- Stop loss: position has significant unrealized loss

### When to OPEN:
- No position exists for that coin AND signal is strong
- Check the Signal Scoring Analysis for direction guidance

## IMPORTANT RULES
- ONE position per coin maximum (can't have both long AND short on same coin)
- Check "Current Positions" before deciding - don't open if already have position on that coin
- Check "open_positions" in portfolio data for existing positions
- Prefer HOLD over frequent open/close to avoid overtrading and fees
- leverage: use 1-3x for normal signals, 4-7x for strong signals, 8-10x only for very strong conviction
- Consider overall portfolio: don't put all capital in one position
